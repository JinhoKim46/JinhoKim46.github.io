## Problem

Clinical 2× accelerated, respiratory-triggered MRCP takes 599 s at 3 T and 542 s at 0.55 T on average. Going faster needs better reconstruction, and fully-sampled MRCP training data is practically impossible to get.

## Approach

Unrolled networks were trained on 3 T data retrospectively undersampled six-fold, either supervised against the 2× scans or self-supervised with no reference. They were then tested on prospective 6× scans and on 0.55 T data without retraining.

## Implementation

Thirty-five healthy volunteers at 3 T and 0.55 T. Baselines were parallel imaging (GRAPPA, CG-SENSE) and compressed sensing, compared on PSNR and SSIM.

## Results

Deep learning reconstructions had higher PSNR and SSIM than parallel imaging and CS in both retrospective and prospective tests. Acquisition time fell from 599 s to 255 s at 3 T (2.4×) and from 542 s to 180 s at 0.55 T (3.0×), and the 3 T-trained models held up at 0.55 T.
