## Problem

In this study, 2× respiratory-triggered MRCP took 599 s at 3 T and 542 s at 0.55 T on average. Further acceleration requires effective reconstruction, but fully sampled MRCP training data is impractical.

## Approach

Unrolled networks used 3 T data retrospectively undersampled six-fold. Supervised training used 2× images as targets; self-supervised training used none. Testing used prospectively acquired 6× scans and 0.55 T data without retraining.

## Implementation

35 healthy volunteers were scanned: 31 at 3 T and 4 at 0.55 T. Comparators were CG-SENSE and CS. Retrospective PSNR/SSIM used 2× GRAPPA images as references.

## Results

Deep learning reconstructions had higher PSNR/SSIM than PI and CS in retrospective tests. Mean scan time fell from 599 to 255 s at 3 T and from 542 to 180 s at 0.55 T. Models trained at 3 T also reconstructed 0.55 T data without retraining.
