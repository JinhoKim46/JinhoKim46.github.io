## Problem

A MIP displays the highest signal along each ray. Bright gastric or duodenal fluid can obscure the ducts.

## Approach

A 3D nnU-Net segments background, primary and secondary structures. Safety margins reduce the risk of duct omission; MIPs use the retained volume.

## Implementation

322 MRCP scans from 162 patients were collected. Training used 265 scans from 127 patients; evaluation used one scan each from 35 patients. Two radiologists rated original and processed MIPs on a 4-point scale; Wilcoxon tests used Benjamini–Hochberg correction.

## Results

Overlap ratings rose from 2.5 ± 0.6 to 3.5 ± 0.9; diagnostic confidence rose from 3.2 to 3.4. Both changes were significant. Code is not public.
