## Problem

A MIP shows only the brightest signal along each ray. Bright fluid in the stomach or duodenum lands on top of the ducts and makes the image hard to read.

## Approach

A 3D nnU-Net segments background, primary and secondary structures. Generous safety margins keep ducts from being cut, and only the remaining volume is projected.

## Implementation

322 3D MRCP datasets from 162 patients: 265 cases from 127 patients for training, 35 single-acquisition cases for evaluation. Two board-certified radiologists rated original and processed MIPs on a 4-point scale; Wilcoxon tests with Benjamini–Hochberg correction.

## Results

The structure-overlap score rose significantly from 2.5 ± 0.6 to 3.5 ± 0.9, and diagnostic confidence went from 3.2 to 3.4. The code is not public.
